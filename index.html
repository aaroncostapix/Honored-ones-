Goa Transit Accessibility Context — Why SAFESTOP?
BIT N BUILD ACCESS TRACK EVIDENCE

A transit vehicle can be accessible while the boarding environment at the selected stop is not. SAFESTOP addresses this gap by combining vehicle accessibility, stop accessibility, verification freshness, and community barrier reports to estimate Boarding Confidence.

📋 June 2026 RTI Findings 470 of 515 KTCL buses lacked wheelchair ramps; 399 lacked audio announcements & display screens. Only 7 of 16 bus stands were identified as disability-friendly.
⚠️ April 2026 Smart City Incidents Wheelchair users stranded due to broken ramps and high curb blocks on Panaji Smart City electric buses, demonstrating that bus capability alone is insufficient.
⚖️ July 2026 High Court Action Bombay High Court proceedings initiated following visually impaired passenger complaints regarding disabled audio announcements, mandating transit accessibility improvements.
💡 Data Honesty Note: The findings above document official Goa transit accessibility evidence. Accessibility scores, stop profiles, and barrier reports in this prototype are simulated demo data created to demonstrate the SAFESTOP methodology.
